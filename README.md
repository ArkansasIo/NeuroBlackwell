# NeuroBlackwell

A research platform for mathematical computing, spiking-neuron simulation, biological-computing research, procedural environments, and reproducible experiments.

## Research status

This repository combines an educational neural simulator with mathematical and experiment tooling. Simulated results must not be represented as measurements of living neural cultures. External projects such as Cortical Labs CL1/CL SDK are integrated by documented adapters and references, subject to their own licenses and documentation.

## Initial components

- Optimized educational neural simulator
- Modular mathematics engine
- Algebra, calculus, probability, differential equations, linear algebra, geometry, graph theory, optimization, and discrete mathematics
- Procedural maze and odd/even benchmark design
- Research literature and computational experiments
- MySQL/PHP research toolchain
- Integration references for Cortical Labs, Brian2, NEST, NEURON, Nengo, Norse, SpikingJelly, snnTorch, and BindsNET

See `docs/` for architecture and upstream integration notes.
